# Refund Policy

## Refund window
The refund window is 30 days. For delivered orders the 30 days start on the delivery date. For orders that have not been delivered, the 30 days start on the order date.

## Delayed or undelivered orders
If a shipped order is more than 3 days past its expected delivery date, the customer is eligible for a full refund of the order total. A missing tracking record does not prove that a parcel is lost. In that case the case must be escalated to a support agent.

## Damaged, missing or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, is missing from the parcel, or is not the item that was ordered, the customer can ask for a refund within the refund window.

## Delivered orders with no problem
Orders that arrived on time and have no problem are not refunded directly. The customer can use the return process instead (see the return policy).

## Orders that are not refundable
- Orders that are already refunded cannot be refunded a second time.
- Orders that were cancelled have nothing to refund.
- Only one refund request may exist for each order.

## Approval and payment
Every refund must be approved by a human support agent before any money is returned. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually arrive in 5 to 7 business days.
