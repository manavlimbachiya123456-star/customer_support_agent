# Customer Support FAQ

## How do I check my order status?
Give the support assistant your order ID (for example ORD-1042). It will show the status and the expected delivery date.

## How long do refunds take?
After a support agent approves a refund it usually reaches your original payment method in 5 to 7 business days.

## Can I get a refund and a replacement?
No. Choose one. A refund closes the order.

## What if I do not have my order ID?
The assistant can list your recent orders so you can choose the right one.

## How do I talk to a human?
Ask the assistant to pass your case to a support agent. Cases with missing data or unclear policy are also passed to a human automatically.

## Is my data safe?
Support staff and the assistant can only see the orders on your own account.
