# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships. Express delivery takes 1 to 3 business days. The expected delivery date is shown on the tracking page.

## Delayed shipments
A shipment is treated as delayed when it passes its expected delivery date. If a shipment is more than 3 days overdue, the customer may ask for a refund (see the refund policy). Delays are usually caused by carrier problems, weather or address checks.

## Tracking
Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after a parcel is handed to the carrier. Missing tracking updates do not mean the parcel is lost.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can only be changed before the order ships. After shipping, the address cannot be changed. Address changes need approval from a support agent.

## Lost parcels
A parcel is only treated as lost after the support team has confirmed it with the carrier.
